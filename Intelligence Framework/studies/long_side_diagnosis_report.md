# Long-side diagnosis: why 16 of 16 open-market decisions were SHORT

Week-of-10/17 item 3. **Diagnosis only.** Nothing on the decision path was changed and no threshold was moved. Generated by `studies/long_side_diagnosis.py`: the funnel ran on local files, read-only; the mirror test ran on real OANDA bars through `scripts/reality_run.py`, and the project hash was unchanged. Prices printed before `6471072` (2026-09-30T08:54Z) were the previous daily close, so none is treated as observed here. Regime labels come from completed bars and are unaffected.

## Verdict

**No mechanical directional defect: a window artifact, localized.** The long side was lost **upstream, at the daily regime classification**.
- In 179 market-open classifications (2026-06-28 → 2026-10-16), daily TRENDING_UP occurred **0 times on either pair**. TRENDING_DOWN occurred 14 times (GBP/AUD) and 22 times (GBP/JPY).
- TRENDING_UP needs daily ADX ≥ 25 **and** confirmed higher highs and higher lows. GBP/AUD's daily structure did confirm UPTREND 21 times, but its daily ADX was below 25 in 20 of the 21. GBP/JPY's was below 25 in 9 of 9.
- **In the rally window (from 2026-09-25), GBP/AUD's daily ADX never exceeded 19.3.**
- The path itself is mirror-symmetric.
  - On 600 real as-of points, every decision mirrored exactly.
  - A planted one-sided rule was detected by the same test.
  - On ~3 years of real bars the same code issues LONGs: 16 LONG / 5 SHORT on GBP/AUD and 18 / 3 on GBP/JPY, counted as sampled points, not episodes.

**What would change this verdict:** a live window in which daily structure confirms UP with daily ADX ≥ 25 and no LONG follows. The mirror test says such a window would produce one.

**Hypothesis recorded, not tested:** a symmetric ADX gate passes fewer rallies than falls if rallies grind and falls are sharp ("up the stairs, down the elevator"). The 3-year sample above leans against this for these pairs.

- `_detect_structure` pivots and confirmation (`:348-412`).
- `_compute_adx`: +DM and −DM use identical strict tests (`:486-499`).
- `_trend_dir`: ±0.5% (`:582-586`).
- `_detect_conflict` (`:543-546`).
- RESOLUTION_TABLE: all 16 cells mirror in mode, confidence and size (checked programmatically).
- `no_trade_filter._derive_direction` (`:80-104`), the weekly gate (`:327-347`), the opposite-trend block (`:226`), the exhaustion stand-aside (`:211-221`), and the override (`:393-461`), whose bullish and bearish reads are each equivalent to `lt_trend`.
- `official_decision.decide`:
  - the override direction from H4 (`:92-100`);
  - the range edges (`:113-116`, disabled anyway);
  - the structure bonus (`:151-156`), the ADX bonus, the exhaustion penalty and the GBP/JPY ATR-ratio penalty (both directions);
  - the override cap and the grade thresholds.

**Empirical check.** Real bars were reflected about their mean, which turns every up move into the same-sized down move while leaving ATR, ADX, pivots and pip distances unchanged, and run through the real `get_regime` and official decision (section below).
- Direction mismatches: **0 of 600**.
- The remaining differences are of two kinds:
  - **The label table (#1).**
  - **A level artifact of the reflection.** The ±0.5% weekly threshold is symmetric, but reflecting the bars changes the price level, so the same move reads as a different percentage. Every such case is an original move inside ±0.5% (0.32–0.50% in magnitude) whose mirrored magnitude grows by about 1.2–1.7x and crosses 0.5% (e.g. +0.448% → −0.554%). None changed a direction.
- **Positive control:** with `TRENDING_UP` planted to require ADX ≥ 27, the same run reported 3 (GBP/AUD) and 8 (GBP/JPY) direction mismatches, plus regime and conviction mismatches. The plant was reverted; the diff is empty and caches were cleared.
