**Hindsight warning.** A move's size is measured from the exact turn to the exact turn, which nobody can know at the time. It is NOT a profit that was available. This report is descriptive: it shows which kinds of move the framework stood aside from, not what trading them would have earned.

## Methods (technical)

- Zig-zag on H4 mid closes, reversal threshold 1 x daily ATR(14) (Wilder, regime_detection.compute_atr_series), ATR in force = last D candle closed at or before the H4 bar's close. Moves = legs >= 2 x the ATR in force at the leg's start bar. The leg after the last confirmed swing is reported as still running.
- Runs: regime_classification_log.csv rows, brief day-log blocks, trade_signal_log.csv rows; matched within 10 min; re-runs within 10 min collapsed. A run is in force for at most 12 h. Decision of record: brief DECISION > trade signal > regime stand_down (True = stand aside; False = not recorded).
- Exclusions: exclusions.excluder() applied to the regime log, the trade signal log and the shadow log; brief blocks matching an excluded regime row are dropped.
- Sources: regime_classification_log.csv (248 rows, 1 excluded), trade_signal_log.csv (13 rows, 1 excluded), shadow_signal_log.csv (36 rows, 0 excluded), 586 pair blocks from /Users/quis/Intelligence Framework/logs (0 blocks skipped: unknown header zone).
