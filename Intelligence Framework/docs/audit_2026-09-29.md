# Full framework audit — 2026-09-29

Six passes: five adversarial reviewers working independently (live decision path, evidence/grading loop, dead code, quant value, tests/security/ops), plus a `/code-review` of the unmerged `feat/exclusion-registry` branch. All were read-only against the real ledgers. Scratch reproductions ran on synthetic data or temp copies. Ledger mtimes were checked before and after, and nothing was written.

Findings are marked **[V]** where the lead reproduced them directly and **[R]** where a reviewer reproduced them with a scratch script or a live read-only OANDA call.

---

## 1. Verdict

**Value today: low as a trading tool, moderate as a foundation, high as a record of honest method.**

What is genuinely good and worth keeping:
- **The honesty culture.** Non-overlapping N, INFORMED PRIOR versus VALIDATED, negative results logged, and self-reported contamination. Most retail systems have none of this.
- **Mechanics.** Candle handling has no look-ahead, the ADX/ATR math is correct Wilder, and the shadow-grading R arithmetic is correct.
- **Pre-registration.** Frozen candidate specs with fresh ledgers on any change.

What makes it low-value today:
- **Evidence.** One graded episode exists (EARLY_MOMENTUM_v2, about −0.55R). Zero real trades have ever been logged.
- **Time to a verdict.** At the observed firing rates, the N=30 FLAG is 2027–2028 and the N=60 DECISION is 2028–2030.
- **Even then the verdict is weak.** N=60 has 16–36% power to detect a realistic edge of 0.10–0.20R, and a zero-edge candidate "passes" 17–28% of the time.
- **The live brief is mostly "stand aside."** It says STAND ASIDE about 90% of the time. When it does give a direction, there is no stop, no target, and sometimes a size that contradicts `risk_config`: grade C is shown with "75% size" while risk_config defines C as NO TRADE.
- **The structural flaw.** What you can act on (the live brief) cannot be scored in R. What can be scored in R (the shadow candidates) is not something you trade.
- **Nothing grades you.** For a goal of "grow and elevate as a trader", that is the missing half.
- **Governance outweighs output.** About 38k lines (code, tests, docs) and 23 specs sit behind a system that has produced one graded episode. The process is aimed at ledger hygiene, but the binding constraint is sample size.

## 2. Critical and high defects (fix before trusting any number)

### Evidence integrity

1. **[V] The price is up to about 24 hours stale, and the ledgers record the stale price.**
   - **Mechanism:** `oanda_feed._get_bars` (`oanda_feed.py:208`) drops the forming bar. `regime_detection._split_live_bar` (`:764`) assumes the forming bar is still there, so it never splits, and `current_price` is always the previous D1 close.
   - **Where the stale price goes:** `trade_signal_log.price_at_signal` (used as the grading entry price), `regime_classification_log.price_at_log`, and the brief's `Price:` line.
   - **Real example:** three GBP_JPY SHORT rows on 2026-09-25 all carry 209.984 while their context cites 209.2–209.6.
   - **Live check [R]:** the brief showed 1.8938 while the live mid was 1.8977.
   - **Fix:** key the split on the `complete` flag, or use `get_live_price()` for the displayed and logged price.
2. **[R] Regime-accuracy tracking measures nothing.**
   - MR_WIN compares against the mean of the outcome window itself, so a RANGING call is "correct" whatever price does.
   - VOL_EXPANSION is nearly impossible to pass (GBP_AUD 0/12).
   - "Independent" N is inflated about 2x (17 shown, 9 real D1 states).
   - This feeds DATA BASIS in the brief. Pull it from display until it is re-specified.
3. **[R] Shadow episode counting overcounts N** (`shadow_signals.py:443`).
   - A 30-bar horizon is treated as 120 clock hours. It is really about 168h, because of weekends.
   - The count also includes ungraded rows.
4. **[R] Shadow candidates are only evaluated on about half the H4 bars.** The bars at 01Z and 13Z are never checked, which halves the firing rate and biases the sample by session.
5. **[R] The trade-signal grader** scores direction only (no stop), from the stale entry price, with no deduplication: one decision logged three times counts as N=3. Its only graded row (2026-06-28, exactly +150.0 pips) looks synthetic, and validation_log:559 admits synthetic data was injected that day.
6. **[R] Integration tests write synthetic rows into the real ledgers** (`test_signal_acceptance.py:120`, `test_brief_display.py:157`). One `pytest -m integration` run appends a fake grade-A LONG to `trade_signal_log.csv`. The 2026-07-29T04:23:31Z "test-fixture signature" row has the same shape, which likely explains that unexplained row.
7. **[R] No file locking; graders rewrite whole files with `open("w")`.** A brief append that lands during a grader rewrite is lost. A crash mid-write truncates the ledger, and there are no backups.
8. **[R] `session_context_log.csv` prunes itself to 30 days**, which contradicts "append-only". The exclusion registry entry for 2026-09-16 will point at a deleted row after about 2026-10-16.

### Decision logic

9. **[R] GBP/JPY H4 exhaustion becomes a grade-A pullback LONG.** The H4 EXHAUSTION subtype returns RANGING, and `RESOLUTION_TABLE[(TRENDING_UP, RANGING)]` reads that as PULLBACK_ENTRY. The RANGING overload that AGENTS.md treats as future work is already live through this table cell.
10. **[R] A failed weekly fetch silently disables the 20-week trend gate.** It defaults to RANGING, which the gate skips. H4 has an UNAVAILABLE sentinel; weekly does not.
11. **[R] ADX ≥ 32 calls a trend from slope alone, with no structure confirmation, at full size.** Tests T10, T11 and T15 pin this behavior in place.
12. **[R] The conflict hard block is dead.** The filter checks for the token `TRENDING_UP__TRENDING_DOWN`, which is never emitted (the real tokens are `D1_BULLISH_H4_BEARISH` and its mirror). The protected floor test feeds the phantom token.
13. **[R] `apply_filter` is not the single stand-down authority.** (RANGING, RANGING) and (RANGING, TRENDING_*) pass the filter and are then vetoed by `official_decision`. The ledgers record `filter_allowed=True` on stand-aside briefs, and session memory announces "conditions improved".
14. **[R] Fail-open gates:**
    - The news gate judges staleness by the newest event, not by coverage, so last week's calendar reads as "all clear".
    - Missing level data loosens the M15 trigger.
    - Every OANDA error becomes `None`, shown as "not enough data", and the retry never fires.
15. **[R] Market hours are hard-coded at 21:00 UTC** (wrong in winter, when the open and close are at 22:00), and holidays are ignored.

### Operations

16. **[V] The weekly heartbeat has never run under launchd.** `/bin/bash` 3.2 can't parse the apostrophe at `weekly_heartbeat.sh:35`, and three Sundays have been lost. **Fixed on `chore/audit-cleanup`**, with a guard test.
17. **[R] The health channel flaps daily.** One failure counter is shared by both jobs, and the stale `storylines.md` (a file you update by hand) fails every brief. The result is an ALERT followed by "RECOVERED after 3 failed runs" every day, which is the rule-8 anti-pattern. Meanwhile a grader that fails every night is never alerted.
18. **[R] The outcome wrapper records a Python traceback as success**, and the brief wrapper throws away logger stderr.
19. **[R] The scheduled jobs are running unmerged, protected code.** The checkout has been on `feat/exclusion-registry` since 2026-09-16, and launchd runs whatever is checked out. Run launchd from a dedicated `main` worktree.
20. **[R] The environment can't be reproduced.** Every requirement is `>=` (pandas resolved to 3.x), pytest is missing, and shadow-only packages are listed. The venv's Python is managed by Homebrew, so a `brew upgrade` can break it.

### Security

21. **[V] A GitHub personal access token is stored in plaintext** in `.claude/settings.local.json` and in `~/.zsh_history`. It is not in git history (verified). **Rotate it**: it has also passed through this audit session.
22. **[R] The git repository root is your entire home directory.** Only project files are tracked today, but about 120 untracked home entries, including shell history holding the token, are one `git add -A` away. The agent allowlist includes `git add *`, `git push *` and `python3 -c *`.
    - **Proper fix:** `git filter-repo --subdirectory-filter "Intelligence Framework"` on a fresh clone.
    - **Interim fix:** a `~/.gitignore` containing `/*` and `!/Intelligence Framework/`.
23. **[R] The OANDA key has full trading scope and defaults to the live environment.** It is also exported in `~/.zshrc`. Run `chmod 600 .env` and remove the export from your shell profile.
24. **[R] `.mcp.json` auto-starts an unpinned `npx -y @modelcontextprotocol/server-github`** with the token in its environment.

## 5. Roadmap: what makes this valuable (ranked by value per effort)

1. **Fix evidence integrity first (S–M).**
   - Stale price (#1).
   - Episode counting (#3).
   - File locking plus atomic writes plus nightly ledger backup (#7).
   - An autouse conftest fixture that redirects every ledger path, plus a session guard that hashes the real ledgers before and after (#6).
   - Per-job health counters, with `storylines.md` taken out of failure classification (#17).
   - Until these land, every tally is suspect.
2. **Run a proper historical test on OANDA's own 2014–2021 data (M, 1–3 days).**
   - The "history validation INFEASIBLE" verdict covers only the third-party 2005–2013 span.
   - `studies/gbpaud_personality_profile.py` already pages OANDA history with `from`/`count`.
   - One pre-registered, cost-aware walk-forward run of the frozen candidates gives about 200/130/70 episodes: years of forward ledger in days. It will most likely retire them, which is also an answer.
3. **Rewrite the decision rules around statistical power (S).**
   - Pre-register a lower-confidence-bound test, for example a 90% CI on avg R above 0, with the N it actually needs (hundreds).
   - Correct for testing 5 candidate/pair cells.
   - Drop the N=60 / 0.15R rule, which is a coin flip.
4. **Grade the trader, not only the model (S–M). This is the growth engine.**
   - Every `pre_trade_journal` idea and every real trade gets scored in R.
   - Keep a weekly review: you versus the framework versus the candidates, plus a rule-adherence log.
   - Take candidate fires at micro size on an OANDA practice account to get real fills and execution practice.
   - This produces N from your own decisions, which is what "grow and elevate" actually needs.
5. **Make the live brief a complete trade plan, or demote it to "context" (M).**
   - Entry trigger, stop, target and invalidation, so it can be scored in R.
   - Resolve the grade-C-with-75%-size contradiction.
   - Fix the logic defects #9–#14.
6. **Put spread and financing into the grader (S).** GBP/JPY swap is about 0.1R per 5-day hold, the same size as the edge being hunted.
7. **Widen the universe to get sample size (M–L).** Build one cost-aware backtest harness and test documented FX premia (1–12-month time-series momentum and carry) across the G10 crosses OANDA offers. Two pairs with selective H4 rules is sample starvation by design.
8. **Cut governance to fit one operator (S).**
   - Apply the removal manifest.
   - Freeze display and notification specs.
   - Keep spec-first review for the tradeable path only.
   - Stop adding a standing rule after every incident: fix the class of bug with a test instead.
   - Run launchd from a `main` worktree so unmerged branches never run live.

### Stop doing

- Treating the forward ledger as the only route to proof.
- Inventing candidates from a handful of circled moves.
- Hand-tuning conviction integers.
- Writing new display or UX specs while the evidence path is unsound.
- Keeping 9,000 lines of quarantined code green under test.
