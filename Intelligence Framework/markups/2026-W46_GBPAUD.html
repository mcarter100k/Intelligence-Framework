How to follow this chart

  • Start on Weekly, then Daily, then 4-hour. That's the top-down order: the bigger picture first, timing last.
  • Shaded bands are zones, named by position: S1–S3 below price and R1–R3 above, with 1 nearest. The darker bands came from the weekly chart and carry more weight.
  • Dashed lines mark where each timeframe's read would be wrong. They're the most important lines on the chart, because they're where your idea dies.
  • A zone is where to pay attention, not where to buy or sell. In the framework's tests, trading the touch of a level won half the time with a typical result of −1R. Wait for the timeframe below to turn.

How Friday is scored (set before the week)

Scoring runs from Mon Nov 9, 7:33 PM PST to Fri Nov 13, 2:00 PM PST. Each zone gets one result:

  • Reached: price traded into it.
  • Held: after reaching it, price moved away at least 62 pips (half a daily range) before a daily close through it.
  • Broke: a daily close through the far side.
  • Never reached.

All four results count. About 58% of bars sit near some level, so hits alone prove nothing.
